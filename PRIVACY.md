# Privacy Policy for OTD Price

**Last Updated:** October 6, 2026

## Overview
**OTD Price** is committed to absolute privacy. The extension was designed from day one with a strict local-first architecture: **no data ever leaves your device**.

## What Data We Collect
**Nothing.**
OTD Price does **not** collect, track, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, search terms, IP addresses, or device identifiers.

## How Data Is Stored
All user preferences (such as your chosen home state override and per-site enable/disable toggles) are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage via `chrome.storage.local`.
- These settings never leave your machine.
- They are not synced to external servers or third parties.
- If you uninstall the extension, this local storage is automatically erased by your browser.

## No Network Requests
All automotive tax and fee calculations run 100% locally using a static, bundled dataset derived from CarTaxHub.
The extension makes **zero** outbound HTTP/HTTPS requests to any external server or API. You can verify this anytime using the Chrome DevTools Network tab.

## Third-Party Services & Analytics
OTD Price contains:
- **No analytics SDKs** (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, etc.)
- **No telemetry** or crash reporting services
- **No advertising code** or tracking pixels
- **No external scripts** or CDNs

## Open Source
OTD Price is fully open source. You can audit the complete source code, manifest, and calculation logic on GitHub.

## Changes to This Policy
Because OTD Price does not collect data, our privacy principles will not change. If any updates are made to support future browser requirements, they will be documented in our public release notes.
