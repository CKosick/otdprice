Privacy Policy for OTD Price

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Local-First Architecture OTD Price is built on a simple foundation: trust. The extension runs entirely inside your browser. No data leaves your device, ever.

1. Information We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, car search queries, IP addresses, or device analytics.

2. Local Storage

Your user preferences (such as your home state override or site enable/disable switches) are stored locally in your browser via Chrome's isolated storage API (chrome.storage.local). This data is never sent across the internet.

3. Zero Outbound Network Requests

All tax calculations and state fee estimates run locally using a static, bundled dataset from CarTaxHub. The extension never contacts remote servers or external APIs.

4. No Analytics or Third Parties

OTD Price contains no telemetry SDKs, no tracking pixels, no advertising code, and no third-party libraries.

5. Open Source

The entire source code is public and auditable on GitHub at github.com/cartaxhub/otd-price.

6. Contact

For questions or support, reach out to the CarTaxHub team at cartaxhub.com.
